# Engineering Milestones

This timeline summarizes major engineering themes. It intentionally omits commit identifiers, implementation details, deployment addresses, transaction records, and private operational information.

## 1. Platform foundation

- Built the multi-role product foundation for creators, brands, agencies, communities, and administrators
- Established authentication, profiles, reputation, missions, notifications, and wallet-aware reward experiences
- Created the product structure for CreatorFi, SocialFi, and GameFi. Earlier LegalFi and RWAFi work was later disabled

## 2. Arbitrum settlement and rewards

- Developed campaign and mission funding lifecycles with release and refund handling
- Added support for ETH, USDC, and ARB reward contexts
- Built transaction status, reconciliation, replay prevention, and failure-recovery patterns
- Expanded reputation, attestation, revenue accounting, and chain-facing validation

## 3. Creator intelligence

- Expanded the creator-data surface toward eleven platforms
- Brought seven platform adapters into the documented live snapshot
- Built confidence, explainability, discovery, matching, anti-abuse, and semantic-search experiences
- Added a ten-dimension intelligence model while keeping its weights and formulas private

## 4. AI-assisted product operations

- Developed more than ten specialized agents across creator, legal, partner, and operational workflows
- Added confidence-aware outputs and human-review boundaries
- Built document intelligence, retrieval, coverage-gap handling, and expert-review escalation
- Created an AI assistant layer for product and ecosystem guidance

## 5. Robinhood Chain expansion

- Added a separate Robinhood Chain product context
- Developed creator-ecosystem, rewards, claims, discovery, and market-intelligence experiences
- Added chain-specific reliability, balance, routing, and monitoring work
- Built safety screening and optional automation with explicit risk controls

## 6. Trading intelligence and automation

- Developed signal ingestion, position lifecycle, monitoring, and staged exit concepts
- Added user configuration, safety checks, duplicate prevention, and recovery states
- Expanded observability around pool availability, chain reliability, and execution outcomes
- Kept proprietary scoring, thresholds, routing logic, and safety heuristics private

## 7. Reliability, security, and governance

- Hardened financial workflows around idempotency, reconciliation, and explicit confirmation
- Added staged release validation and production-readiness gates
- Improved privacy, consent, KYC, and disclosure controls
- Separated public product evidence from private implementation and operational material

## Continuing work

TogetherFi remains an active engineering program. A documented capability may be experimental, test-environment only, partner-gated, or in staged rollout. This record demonstrates engineering breadth and sustained activity, not a promise that every capability is generally available.
